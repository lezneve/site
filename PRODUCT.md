# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), no client framework. Chosen by the user. Deploy target not decided.

## Users

people on windows who like small, quiet tools and want to see what a tool does, what it costs while idle, and where to download it, in under a minute. some read the source.

## Product Purpose

the public site of lezneve, a maker of small windows software: introduces the maker and its two projects, kadr (screenshots) and ryad (text editor), and sends people to the download. success: a visitor understands each tool in one screen and finds the installer without searching.

## Positioning

"small software. carefully made." the difference is absence: no accounts, no network, no telemetry, measured idle cost stated as facts. the site shows the same restraint.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- pages: home (lezneve + the two projects), one page per project (kadr, ryad).
- content is taken from each project's README; all numbers come from the projects' measurements (one machine, stated as such).
- language: english, lowercase where natural.
- downloads link to github releases (github.com/lezneve/kadr, github.com/lezneve/ryad). both are windows 10/11 x64, MIT.
- analytics: only vercel web analytics on this site (cookieless, first-party script, no personal data). the apps themselves stay free of telemetry. no other cookies, no third-party requests, self-hosted fonts.

## Brand Commitments

- maker: lezneve. voice: lowercase, short, calm, no superlatives, no cute phrasing.
- kadr's own design (graphite, ink on ground, crop marks) is the nearest existing identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- measurements and behaviour in kadr/README.md, ryad/README.md and their docs/.
- no screenshots, logos beyond the .ico files, testimonials or user numbers exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. say what it does, plainly, then stop.
2. claims are measured; unknowns are stated.
3. the site costs the visitor as little as the tools cost the machine.
